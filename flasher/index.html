  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Other browsers cannot open serial ports.
  2. Plug the board in with a USB cable and press any button, so it is on. The port is gone while the board is off.
  3. Press Install and pick the port named USB JTAG/serial debug unit, not the USB Single Serial one.
  4. Wait for it to finish (about half a minute). The board restarts by itself.
This browser cannot flash the board. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Flashing needs a secure page (https).

Your profile and friends live on the SD card and are not touched. The battery gauge starts over after flashing.

Windows 10 and later need no driver for this port.
